Cloudflare Tunnels

Running a Virtual Private Server (VPS) often means locking down your firewall to keep unwanted traffic out. However, what happens when you build a web app, a portfolio, or a personal dashboard that you want the public to see?

Traditionally, that meant opening inbound firewall ports and managing tedious SSL certificates. Fortunately, Cloudflare offers a free tool called Cloudflare Tunnel that completely changes the game.

Instead of punching holes in your server's security perimeter, Cloudflare Tunnel lets your VPS securely initiate an outbound connection to Cloudflare's global edge network. When a visitor wants to access your web service, Cloudflare safely routes their request through this encrypted tunnel. This means your VPS firewall can remain tightly shut to the outside internet.

Your actual server IP address stays entirely hidden, protecting you from direct DDoS attacks. Best of all, you get free automatic HTTPS encryption and custom domain routing without spending a dime.

Setting it up takes just a few minutes using their lightweight cloudflared daemon. It is arguably the easiest, most secure way to share your private server's creations with the world.
